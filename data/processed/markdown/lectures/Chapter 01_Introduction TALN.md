## Slide / Page 4

NLP
• Goal of NLP: get computers to perform useful tasks
involving human language, e.g.:
 enabling human-machine communication
 improving human-human communication
 doing useful processing of text or speech, etc.
• Conversational agents (e.g. HAL 9000 computer in
Stanley Kubrick’s film 2001: A Space Odyssey;
Apple SIRI, Amazon Alexa, …)
 Analyse language input (Automatic Speech
Recognition and N.L. Understanding)
 Analyse language output (NL generation and Speech
Synthesis).
• Machine Translation

## Slide / Page 5

NLP (cont.)
• Web-based Question Answering
 Definition questions
 Factoid questions.
 More complex questions (How, Why):
 information embedded in other text on a Web page,
 doing inference (drawing conclusions based on known facts),
 synthesizing and summarizing information from multiple
sources or web pages.
• Modern understanding systems, including Info.
Extraction, Word Sense Disambiguation
• This chapter summarizes the kinds of knowledge
necessary for these tasks (and others like spell
correction, grammar checking, …) and (throughout

• Phonetics and Phonology: knowledge about
linguistic sounds.
• Morphology: knowledge of the meaningful
components of words.
• Syntax: knowledge of the structural relationships
between words.
• (Lexical & compositional) Semantics : knowledge
of meaning.
• Pragmatics: knowledge of the relationship of
meaning to the goals and intentions of the speaker.
• Discourse: knowledge about linguistic units larger
than a single utterance. E.g. How many states were in
the US that year? (case of coreference resolution)

Models
In NLP different types of models can be
developed and used for different purposes,
whichever is most appropriate:
• State machines (FSM, FST, Weighted, …)
• Production rules (Regular Grammars, CFG,
Feature-Augmented Grammars, …)
• Logical formalisms (FOL, and other logics)
• Probabilistic models
• Vector-Space models (TF-IDF, Bag-of-
Words, Word Embeddings, …)
